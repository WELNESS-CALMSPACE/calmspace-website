5 TIPS FOR WELL-BEING

01
Move Your Body: Walk, stretch, or exercise daily.
02
Stay Hydrated: Drink 6–8 glasses of water daily.
03
Prioritize Sleep: Keep a regular sleep schedule.
04
Stretch Daily: Reduce stiffness and improve posture.
05
Take Breaks: Rest to avoid overtraining or fatigue.
01
Meditate: 5 to 10 minutes daily to reduce stress.
02
Practice Gratitude: Write down 3 things you're thankful for.
03
Breathe Deeply: Use mindful breathing to calm your mind.
04
Journal: Reflect on thoughts and emotions daily.
05
Limit Screen Time: Reduce stress from constant notifications.
01
Plan Your Day: Use a to-do list to structure tasks efficiently.
02
Pomodoro Technique: Work in focused 25-minute intervals with breaks.
03
Prioritize Tasks: Tackle high-impact tasks first to maximize productivity.
04
Limit Multitasking: Focus on one task at a time to reduce errors.
05
Reflect Daily: Review accomplishments and plan improvements for tomorrow.
01
Connect Regularly: Reach out to friends and family weekly.
02
Volunteer: Offer your time or skills to help others in your community.
03
Join Clubs: Participate in clubs or groups to meet like-minded people.
04
Be Present: Give full attention during conversations and interactions.
05
Express Appreciation: Compliment and thank people often to strengthen bonds.
01
Eat Whole Foods: Include vegetables, fruits, and grains in meals.
02
Balanced Diet: Ensure each meal has protein, carbs, and healthy fats.
03
Avoid Processed Sugar: Limit sugary drinks and snacks to improve energy.
04
Snack Smart: Choose nuts, fruits, or yogurt instead of junk food.
05
Eat Mindfully: Chew slowly and focus on your meal to improve digestion.
